## What's in it

1. **The blank canvas.** Print it A3 and fill it in with sticky notes, or type into the editable version.
2. **A worked example.** An AI assistant given travel skills, working for a consultant who visits client sites. Made up, so you can see every box filled without anyone's real data.
3. **How to run it.** Seven steps for the hour, and how each skill on the canvas becomes a job story your team can write, build and test.

## How to use it

- Put the agent in the middle and the person it works for next to it. Everything on the canvas is judged by whether that person ends up better off.
- Frame it first: **Purpose** (what "done well" looks like) and **Boundaries** (what stays with a human, when it must ask, escalate or stop).
- Then the six blocks. **Do, Process, Inform, Explain** become the agent's instructions, its skills. **Consume, Discover, Deliver** become the data and tools it needs, its connections (MCPs).
- Name what it delivers, in what format, and where it lands. If you can't, the outcome isn't clear yet.
- Finish with the three lists on the right: skills to write, connections to build, and how you'll know it's working.

It works for Claude, Copilot and ChatGPT alike, because you aren't building an agent. You're teaching one you already have.

## The editable version

The HTML file opens in any browser. Click a box to type into it; edits save in that browser, and Export JSON keeps or shares them. No account, no upload, nothing leaves your machine.

Free to use, change and pass on. If you'd rather the AI drafted the canvas from your project notes first, the brief builder in the [AI Brief Builder](/tools/ai-brief-builder/) asks the same questions in interview form.
