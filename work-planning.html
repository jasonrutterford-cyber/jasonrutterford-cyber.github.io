Defining how energy investment decisions get planned

One model of an opportunity, from first idea to operation.

Problem
Planning ran across separate tools and teams.
My role
Experience strategy lead for planning products.
What I did
Defined the shared concepts each product builds on.
Delivered
A shared set of planning concepts for the products to build on.
The problem

Five roles. One decision. No shared picture.

Decisions in silos
New choices contradicted earlier ones.
Evidence hard to find
Time lost hunting for data and documents.
No audit trail
Little record of who decided what, or why.
The insight

Every investment follows the same path.

Acquire
Explore
Appraise
Develop
Operate
Identify
Assess
Select
Define
Execute
Five stages of an asset. Five gates inside each one.
The strategy

Make the opportunity the thing everyone works on.

Concept 1
Opportunity register
Every opportunity, ranked in one place.
Concept 2
Studies and scenarios
Compare options before committing.
Concept 3
Decisions and knowledge
Record what was decided, and why.
How I led it
1Built five personas from research
2Mapped the planning lifecycle with domain experts
3Storyboarded how an opportunity matures
4Took the concepts into product design
The value it was built for
Decision quality
Options compared on the same data.
Speed
Less time finding and re-entering information.
Traceability
Every decision linked to its evidence.
Results are confidential to SLB. I can talk through the approach and what it changed.
What I learned

Agree the nouns before the screens. A shared model outlasts any interface.
