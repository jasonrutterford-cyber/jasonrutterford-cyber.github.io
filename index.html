What I write about

What's actually going on

The gap between what gets asked for and what the problem turns out to be. How leaders get to the truth of it quickly, from customers, colleagues and the data already lying around, without a research phase.

Designing the whole service

Not the screen: the process behind it, the hand-offs, the policies and the people who run it. Where to invest, in what order, and what to settle before anyone starts building.

AI in real work

What it can and can't do for an organisation, how to brief for it, and what changes when it does the work a team used to do. Patterns from the inside, never the specifics.

Written by Jason Rutterford. Head of experience inside a large organisation by day; one piece a week here, said plainly, about patterns rather than places.
