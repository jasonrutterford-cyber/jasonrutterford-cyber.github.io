Turning enterprise AI investment into customer value

An experience model that puts AI inside the work.

Problem
AI sat beside the work, not in it.
My role
Head of Experience, Platform and AI. I set the product experience strategy.
What I did
One experience model for every product team.
Delivered
One experience model for SLB enterprise AI that fed 10 products: less design and development effort, and one unified AI experience.
The problem

Capability wasn't the constraint. Work was.

Fragmented work
Split across tools, data and teams.
Expertise that doesn't scale
Held by a few specialists.
Slow decisions
Data rarely became trusted action.
The insight

AI is an ecosystem to work in, not a feature to add.

Isolated toolstoConnected workflows
AI that assiststoAI that executes
Individual know-howtoReusable expertise
Features shippedtoOutcomes delivered
The model

Three layers. Start with a goal, not a tool.

Layer 1
Assistant
Help inside every application.
Layer 2
Workspace
One place to finish a job end to end.
Layer 3
Agents
AI that carries out the task.
The platform provides the experience.
Domains provide the intelligence.
How I led it
1Mapped who it serves
2Traced journeys across products
3Prototyped the experience
4Aligned product and domain leaders
The value it was built for
Adoption
People return for multi-step work.
Productivity
Key workflows take less time.
Differentiation
Expert workflows reused across teams.
What changed: the strategy fed 10 products, reduced design and development effort across them, and gave them one unified experience for AI.
What I learned

Define the decision and the conditions for trust first. Then choose the AI.
